Thump

Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

In short: Thump listens to music on your iPhone to find the beat. Nothing is recorded, stored or sent anywhere. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Microphone

Thump uses the microphone to hear the music around you and vibrate on the beat. The sound is analysed on your iPhone in real time, a few milliseconds at a time, and then thrown away. Thump never records audio, never saves it, and never sends it off your phone.

Crew (nearby phones)

If you start or join a Crew, Thump connects directly to your friends' phones nearby over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth, using Apple's local networking. Only beat timing and light-show colours are shared between the phones in your Crew. Nothing passes through a server, and nothing is shared once you leave the Crew.

Motion

Thump can use the motion sensor to estimate how much you dance, for your recap. This is worked out on your iPhone and never leaves it.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your settings (for example vibration strength).
  • Your latest recap: when you listened, how long, the tempo and the number of beats you felt.
  • The hearing meter's estimate of how loud your day has been.

Deleting Thump deletes all of this.

Notifications

Thump sends notifications from your iPhone itself (for example "Your recap is ready"). No push service is involved.

Flashlight and screen light show

The strobe uses the camera's flashlight only. Thump never takes photos or video.

Children

Thump collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes
