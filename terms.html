What Thump is, and isn't

Thump listens to music through your iPhone's microphone and vibrates on the beat, for entertainment. It is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat or make up for hearing loss and does not replace hearing aids, cochlear implants or any other assistive device.

Hearing estimate

The loudness warnings are a rough estimate made with the phone's microphone, not a measurement. Don't rely on them to protect your hearing. Loud music can damage your hearing. Take breaks and wear earplugs.

Flashing lights

The light show and the strobe flash on the beat. Flashing lights can trigger seizures in people with photosensitive epilepsy and can be uncomfortable for others. Don't use them if you or people around you may be affected, and never point the strobe at someone's eyes. Thump limits flashes to 3 per second and respects the "Dim Flashing Lights" setting, but it can't remove the risk.

Safety

Don't use Thump while driving, cycling, operating machinery or anywhere you need your full attention. Thump pauses itself when CarPlay is connected. Keep an eye on your phone and your surroundings at events.
