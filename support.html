Common questions

It stops vibrating when I lock my phone.

iPhone only lets apps play custom vibrations while they're open. Keep Thump open and put the phone in your pocket: the screen turns off by itself. Don't press the side button.

It misses beats.

Stand closer to the speakers, and keep the bottom of the phone uncovered if you can. Thump locks on to the beat after a few seconds of steady music, then it gets more reliable.

It vibrates when there's no music.

It shouldn't. Thump only vibrates when the music is loud enough and the beat is regular. If it happens, please send us a message and say where you were (club, home, street).

Thump can't hear anything.

Check that microphone access is on: Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone → Thump.

Crew doesn't find my friend's phone.

Both phones need Thump open, Wi-Fi and Bluetooth on, and Local Network access allowed for Thump (Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network).

Is the strobe safe?

The strobe never flashes more than 3 times a second and respects "Dim Flashing Lights". If you're sensitive to flashing lights, don't use it.
